Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

At Connecta, we take data privacy and security seriously. This policy explains how Connecta Technologies Inc. collects, uses, and safeguards personal data provided by clients and freelancers on our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (name, email address, profile photos), identity verification documents, code repository data connected for AI matching, and milestone account transaction logs.

2. Payment Protection

Financial payment data is processed securely through Connecta Milestone Vaults compliant with PCI-DSS standards. Connecta does not store raw credit card numbers on local servers.
